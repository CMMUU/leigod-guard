## hexf-parse provenance

The published crate and its official upstream commit contain no standalone license file. `parse/Cargo.toml` at commit `4225763d744183d720f575ae96d04161b4d08ea0` explicitly declares `license = "CC0-1.0"` and names the author. The original manifest is preserved as `hexf-parse-0.2.1-parse-Cargo.toml.txt`; the separate CC0 text is the official Creative Commons 1.0 legal code, not a file claimed to have existed in the crate repository.
